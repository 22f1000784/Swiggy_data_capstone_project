
# 📊 Swiggy Business Data Analysis – Capstone Project

This repository contains my **capstone project for the Business Data Management course**, focused on analyzing key business challenges faced by **Swiggy**, one of India's leading online food delivery platforms. The project is divided into two main parts: the initial proposal and the final report.

---

## 📁 Project Files

- `Proposal_Report.pdf`:  
  Outlines the initial understanding of Swiggy’s business context, identified challenges, analytical approach, and proposed timeline for execution.
  
- `Final_Report.pdf`:  
  Contains the complete analysis, results, insights, visualizations, and strategic recommendations based on data-driven findings.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To identify operational and customer-related challenges faced by Swiggy using data analysis techniques, and provide actionable recommendations to enhance performance, customer satisfaction, and service efficiency.

---

## 📌 Key Components

- **Problem Identification**:  
  Focused on issues such as low average ratings in specific areas, delayed deliveries, pricing inconsistencies, and customer behavior trends.

- **Analytical Approach**:
  - Descriptive statistics and visualizations
  - Segment-wise rating analysis
  - Correlation and trend analysis
  - Delivery time and price impact study

- **Tools & Technologies**:
  - Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
  - Excel for quick summaries
  - Jupyter Notebook
  - PDF for report compilation

- **Timeline**:
  Proposed and followed an 8–10 day execution plan including data cleaning, analysis, and reporting.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

- Areas with below-average ratings were identified and analyzed.
- Delivery time and price were found to significantly affect customer satisfaction.
- Regional performance variations were highlighted.
- Recommendations were provided for operational improvements and customer engagement.

---

## ✅ Outcome

This project helped develop practical data analysis skills in a real-world business context, offering insights and recommendations that could inform Swiggy’s service improvement strategies.

This project is academic in nature and based on publicly available or synthetically generated data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Swiggy.
